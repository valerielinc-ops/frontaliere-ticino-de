Vollständige Beschreibung

CSC Costruzioni SA , part of the international Webuild Group , has been operating in Switzerland for over 60 years, delivering complex and sustainable construction projects.

With more than 300 employees and 20+ active projects , we combine local expertise with the strength and know-how of one of the world's leading construction groups.

For our major construction project at the United Nations Palais des Nations in Geneva , we are looking for a: Junior Civil Engineer – Internship

Key Responsibilities

Support the Technical Office in the coordination of civil and architectural activities.

Assist in preparing and managing technical documentation, including Method Statements, material and technical submittals, drawings, RFIs, and technical queries .

Maintain and update technical registers, trackers, and project documentation.

Monitor outstanding submissions, comments, and actions, ensuring timely follow-up.

Coordinate with designers, subcontractors, and site teams to collect and exchange technical information.

Support technical meetings by preparing documentation, meeting minutes, and action lists.

Assist in reviewing technical drawings, specifications, subcontractor proposals, and project correspondence.

Provide operational and administrative support to the Technical Office Manager and project team.

Requirements

Bachelor's or Master's degree in Civil Engineering, Construction Engineering, Architecture , or a related discipline (completed or currently in progress).

Basic understanding of construction processes, technical drawings, and project documentation.

Strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and ability to manage multiple tasks and deadlines.

Good knowledge of Microsoft Office ; familiarity with AutoCAD or other technical software is considered an advantage.

Proactive attitude, problem-solving mindset, and willingness to learn.

Ability to work effectively in a multicultural and multidisciplinary environment.

Languages: Fluency in at least two of the following languages: English, Italian, and French . Knowledge of German will be considered an advantage.
