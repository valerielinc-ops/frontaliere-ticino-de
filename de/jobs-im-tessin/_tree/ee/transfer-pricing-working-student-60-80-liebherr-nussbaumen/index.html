Vollständige Beschreibung

To support our Global Transfer Pricing team in Nussbaumen AG, Switzerland, we are looking for a Transfer Pricing Working Student. In this role, you will contribute to international transfer pricing projects and support the implementation of the Group's transfer pricing strategy. Responsibilities:

  • You support to the implementation of the Group's transfer pricing strategy.
  • You assist in the design and monitoring of transfer pricing policies through financial analyses and benchmarking studies.
  • You assist with drafting and reviewing intercompany agreements for cross-border transactions.
  • You contribute to the preparation of transfer pricing documentation and ensure compliance requirements in more than 50 countries.
  • You participate in transfer pricing projects through financial modelling, presentations, reports and monitoring of regulatory developments.
  • You analyse financial and operational data to support transfer pricing policies and business decisions. Competencies:
  • You are studying Economics, Business Administration, Law or a related field.
  • You are fluent in English
  • German is an advantage but not compulsory.
  • You are confident using Microsoft Office and SharePoint.
  • You are able to understand and solve complex analytical problems.
  • You are interested in a multidisciplinary field covering tax, economics, accounting, finance and business operations.
  • You work accurately and pay close attention to detail.
  • You have good communication, teamwork and project management skills.
  • Previous work experience is welcome but not required. Our Offer
  • Join a global market and technology leader with a strong international presence across 13 product segments.
  • Gain practical experience in transfer pricing across more than 50 countries.
  • Be part of an international team working on strategic cross-border projects.
  • Collaborate closely with experts across tax, controlling, treasury, accounting, legal and operations.
  • Benefit from flexible working hours that fit your academic schedule.
