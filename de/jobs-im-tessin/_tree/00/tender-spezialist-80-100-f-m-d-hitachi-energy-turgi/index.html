Vollständige Beschreibung

The opportunity Are you ready to take your career to the next level? We are looking for an experienced, enthusiastic, and highly motivated self-driven tender specialist to strengthen the Global Competence Center Power Conversion team part of Power Grids Grid Integration Business Unit.

In this position you are responsible for tendering the Grid Integration portfolio with focus on Power Conversion (Static Frequency System Applications) Solutions. Your role is to create competitive bids, estimates, and quotations for our innovative solutions. You'll work closely with an interdisciplinary team and external partners to ensure that we offer the best solutions to meet our customers' needs around the globe.

With a pioneering technology-leading offering, Hitachi Energy's Power Conversion Solutions support the green energy transition with solutions that bring resilience, stability and increased capacity to the power grid and consumers. As a result, more renewable energy sources can be integrated, environmental impacts minimized and lower the risk of disruption in the grid. At Hitachi Energy, we value our people as our greatest asset, as we leverage diverse thoughts, backgrounds, and experiences to foster a culture of innovation and collaboration. With our skilled and global project teams, we are there to support our customers worldwide to meet their sustainable goals, paving the way for a more sustainable, flexible, and secure energy system. Join our team!

We offer an exciting opportunity for a passionate person in a dynamic environment who also likes to work hands on.

How you´ll make an impact

  • Understand and empathize with Customer requirements and needs during the acquisition phase
  • Shape innovative sales strategies, selecting winning solutions both commercially and technically
  • Collaborate and coordinate internal and external stakeholders internationally to ensure timely and accurate tender documents. Your attention to detail will be crucial in presenting compelling proposals.
  • Prepare comprehensive, timely, and technically sound commercial offers
  • Ensure seamless integration of Supplier offers risk assessment, mitigation strategies, and tender completeness
  • Bid presentation and technical support during customer meetings.
  • Successfully transition negotiated projects to the project execution and engineering departments

Your background

  • You have previous experience in bid management, sales support, project management or similar.
  • Bachelor's or master's degree in a relevant subject
  • Fluency in English and German. Proficiency in other languages would be an advantage
  • You have great communication skills and the wish to collaborate in a dynamic and multicultural environment
  • Experience in Power Electronics, MC Frequency Converters, Power Quality applications, or Grid Integration is desirable
  • Skilled in collaborating within cross-functional, cross-border teams, while embracing flexible working arrangements
  • Openness to travel approximately 15% of your working time

More about us Join us on this exciting journey as a Tender specialist and contribute to shaping the future of power electronics solutions. We look forward to receiving applications from a diverse range of candidates who share our commitment to innovation and excellence. Apply today to make your mark!

Brauche ich als italienischer Grenzgänger die G-Bewilligung für diese Tessiner Stelle?

Ja. Für EU/EFTA-Bürger hängt die G-Bewilligung nicht mehr von einer Grenzzone ab: Nötig sind ein Wohnsitz in einem EU/EFTA-Staat, ein Schweizer Vertrag und die Rückkehr mindestens einmal pro Woche. Der Schweizer Arbeitgeber beantragt die G-Bewilligung nach Vertragsunterzeichnung beim Migrationsamt des Kantons Tessin — die Erstausstellung dauert 2-6 Wochen. Die Bewilligung gilt fünf Jahre bei unbefristetem oder über einjährigem Vertrag, sonst für die Vertragsdauer.
