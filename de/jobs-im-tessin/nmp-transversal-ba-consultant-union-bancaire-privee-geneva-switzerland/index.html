Mission

The Senior Transversal Business Analyst will support a multi-year banking transformation programme involving the continued implementation of Murex , migration of Asian banking activities into the Bank's strategic core banking platform , migration of Treasury activities into Murex , and insourcing / transformation of Operations activities currently delivered through BPO arrangements in Asia.

  • Mission
  • The Senior Transversal Business Analyst will support a multi-year banking transformation programme involving the continued implementation of Murex , migration of Asian banking activities into the Bank's strategic core banking platform , migration of Treasury activities into Murex , and insourcing / transformation of Operations activities currently delivered through BPO arrangements in Asia.
  • The role operates across individual programme workstreams and provides an end-to-end business perspective , ensuring that business processes, operating models, data, controls and system responsibilities remain coherent across Murex, Core Banking, Operations, Finance, Risk and other impacted functions.
  • The Transversal BA will focus particularly on dependencies and functional boundaries between projects and systems, identifying gaps that may not be visible within individual project workstreams.

  • Treasury / FX product knowledge + Operations knowledge + Murex experience + transformation/business architecture skills.

  • End-to-End Business Analysis: Provide a holistic, end-to-end process view across functions and systems, closing gaps and maintaining critical scenarios.
  • Cross-Programme Dependency Management: Map and manage inter-stream dependencies, escalate conflicts, and optimize change sequencing.
  • Target Operating Model: Define a future operating model with clear responsibilities and sourcing aligned to Murex and Core Banking.
  • Product and Business Process Analysis: Specify front-to-back processing and lifecycles for Treasury products, filtering out legacy-only practices.
  • Systems and Functional Architecture: Assign system-of-records and responsibilities, remove overlaps, and design target interactions and data flows.
  • Data and Migration: Set data ownership and golden sources, define migration rules, and reconcile/validate data across Murex and Core Banking.
  • Controls and Reconciliations: Refresh the control framework, define future reconciliations, automate manual controls, and formalize exceptions.
