Vollständige Beschreibung

Reference code: JR135373

Richemont owns some of the world’s leading luxury goods Maisons, with particular strengths in jewellery, fine watches and premium accessories. Each Maison represents a proud tradition of style, quality and craftsmanship and Richemont seeks to preserve the heritage and identity of each of its Maisons. At the same time, we are committed to innovation and designing new products which are in keeping with our Maisons’ values, through a process of continuous creativity.

YOUR MISSION

As a Program Management Intern, you will join our C&M Program Management Office team within Group Technology and support ongoing projects and operations that strengthen the organisation’s technology foundations. Reporting to the Core Platforms Pole Manager, you will work closely with other team members on specific missions across the team’s portfolio of activities. You will gain hands-on experience in technology services, security, and compliance, while learning how technology enables business operations in a secure and reliable way.

HOW WILL YOU MAKE AN IMPACT?

  • Support of team members across the Core Platforms Pole of Client & Market PMO team.
  • Assist related (Senior) Program Managers in the preparation and execution of program initiative.
  • Contribute to varied activities around structuring and documenting a major Transformation program.
  • Contribute to continuous improvement and develop our internal methodology by updating and maintaining our project documentation.
  • Suggest and organize team activities.

HOW WILL YOU EXPERIENCE SUCCESS WITH US?

  • You are doing a master's degree or are recently graduated (Business or Engineering School) with a strong willingness to enter the area of Technology Innovation and Program Management.
  • You can follow a rapid learning curve and show impactful results (be a doer) after a short discovery phase.
  • You can experience transversal topics from IT to Business areas, see the big picture, and summarize the key elements.
  • You have a client orientated mindset, with an eye for detail and can propose improvements in our quality of deliverables and operational activities.
  • You are effective in communication and can work closely with cross-functional teams.
  • You have a strong team spirit, you are proactive, enthusiastic, and not afraid of new challenges.
  • Eager to master Program Management within an IT environment
  • Skilled in Microsoft 360 (Teams, PowerPoint, Excel & Word), know-how of Atlassian Suite is a bonus.
  • Strong proficiency in English and French is a must.
  • You are available for a 6-month internship starting from January 2027 onwards.
  • You are eligible for an internship agreement.
