Unternehmen und Kontext

  • As an Analyst in the Data & Insights Enablement team, you will join the GATE organization in a pivotal role partnering with the US-based Master Data Management (MDM) team.
  • Your mission is to drive data stewardship, improve data quality, and support critical business decisions.
  • You will act as a bridge between technical data operations and business strategy, ensuring our data assets are accurate, compliant, and actionable.
  • Stewardship: Act as the Data Steward for HCP (Health Care Professional), HCO (Health Care Organization) profile data, and Affiliations.
  • Discrepancy Resolution: Proactively investigate data discrepancies from internal and external sources.
  • Data Integrity: Ensure all newly onboarded datasets are accurate, complete, and strictly adhere to defined data standards. Process Optimization & Governance
  • Governance Methodologies: Develop and document adaptable governance methodologies to enhance efficiency in MDM, data integration, taxonomy, and reporting.
  • Automation: Identify opportunities to automate or improve data management workflows.
  • Workflow Enhancement: Build data products to enhance workflow optimization. Strategic Analysis
  • Analytical Support: Provide analytical support to develop insights and strategies that optimize data-related processes.
