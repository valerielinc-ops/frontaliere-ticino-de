  • You are in charge of the tests to be carried out in the workshop and on the test bench for the validation of the engine components during all the development phases to ensure the passage from prototype to pre-production
  • You analyze the measurement results and report them in various standardized documents
  • You control, validate and share the different reports with the teams concerned at an interdisciplinary level
  • You work in close collaboration with the component technicians
  • You collect test requests and validation plans.

  • You manage the planning of the tests.
