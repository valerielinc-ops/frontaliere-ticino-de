Rollenüberblick

In this role, you are the link between maintenance, pilots and operations management, ensuring that the air force fleet is always ready for deployment and safe. In a dynamic environment, you will be responsible for the following tasks:

  • Maintenance & Repair: Performing scheduled maintenance intervals, including system replacement and testing
  • Repairs & Troubleshooting: Analyzing error messages, troubleshooting processes, and performing structural repairs
  • Aircraft Handover to Pilots: Conducting the final pre-flight check
  • Change Orders, Interim Flight Checks: Visual and functional inspections after each flight (aircraft and system condition)
  • Active Participation in Domestic and International Campaigns: Accompanying pilots/jet crews to training flights
  • Work at Military Airfields: Working at Payerne Military Airfield and at other Swiss military airfields as needed
  • Driver/Logistics Support: Transporting and loading spare parts, tools, and specialized equipment within the airfield
  • Documentation & Quality Management
  • Technical vocational training (EFZ), ideally with an mechanical engineering focus (i.e. polymechanic, automation technician, electronics technician, diagnostician or comparable profession)
  • practical experience and knowledge in flight operations and aircraft maintenance, ideally in a military environment, with the required module qualifications/licenses according to EASA Part-66, Category B1 (Mechanics) or B2 (Avionics) of advantage
  • Category B driver's license mandatory, Category C/E helpful
  • Conversational (technical) Englishskills (min. B1) required; advanced usage of either French and/or German plus versatile digital competences (MS Office/SAP) desirable
  • High level of commitment and flexibility (for QRA, AMA, AMI, night/weekend shifts, and deployment changes both domestically and internationally)
  • Highly resilient, team-oriented personality with a reliable, precise work ethic and a strong sense of responsibility/safety awareness
  • Swiss citizenship (due to working on Swiss Military property) a prerequisite
