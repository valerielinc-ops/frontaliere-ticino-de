Vollständige Beschreibung

Quality by design. Excellence by habit.

Join our team in Corporate Quality Department developing AI methods to continuously improve our products and services. You will be working on new data analysis approaches and collaborate with the global quality experts and cross functional teams. You will have the opportunity to broaden your knowledge and implement applications in practice.

This is a full‑time internship position. Start date is flexible. Duration for at least 6 months. No travel is foreseen for this position.

What You’ll Bring

  • Master student in mechanical or electrical engineering, data science, mathematics or similar
  • Good knowledge of data science and prior experience in AI application
  • Technical affinity
  • Experience in data merging
  • Good communication skill in English.
