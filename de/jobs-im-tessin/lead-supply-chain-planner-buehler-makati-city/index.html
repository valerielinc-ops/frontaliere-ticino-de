Originalbeschreibung

Planning and execution of Supply Chain in Customer Projects. Ensure seamless and accurate scheduling that aligns with business, project, and manufacturing requirements, with particular emphasis on timely delivery. Aufgaben:

  • Convert business information into demand planning within the system, verify capacity across different factories, and ensure financial performance aligns with targets
  • Review quotations to check lead times, consider factory workload in line with the requirements of the business
  • Coordinating closely and collaborating with Commercial team, Procurement, Shipping and Manufacturing
  • Define supply chain events for all components based on project strategy, lead times and given capacities
  • Draft, adjust and update the event schedule and prepare event schedule for kick-off meetings
  • Track major events, monitor progress and proactively define actions with the Project Manager, the Supply Chain and Logistics in case of deviation or delay
  • Act as Single point of contact within the Sales & Operations Planning Process for Project Manager, Supply Chain and Logistics
  • Manage and coordinate factory supplies, ensuring a balanced supply plan that aligns project priorities and factory requirements with the manufacturing planner Anforderungen:
  • Bachelor's Degree in a technical field or equivalent, Master's degree in a technical field will be considered as a plus
  • 1 to 2 years of experience in Demand Planning, Supply Chain Management, or Project Planning
  • Very good communication skills in English, any additional language will be considered as a plus
  • Strong ability in the use of SAP, MS Office (Excel, PowerPoint, Analysis) and SAP IBP
  • Strong customer focused problem solving and conflict management skills
  • Team spirit and well-developed communication skills as well as the ability to work within an interdisciplinary and international environment Two billion people eat food every day that was produced with Bühler equipment.
