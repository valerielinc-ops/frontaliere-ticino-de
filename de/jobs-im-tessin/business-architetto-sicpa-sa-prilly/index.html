Rollenüberblick

We are expanding our Enterprise Architecture practice and seeking a Business Architect to enhance the alignment between our IT application and service portfolio and the evolving needs of the business. In this role, you will ensure that our applications and services deliver optimal value for cost, support informed investment decisions, and contribute to long‑term efficiency and transparency across IT and business domains.

As a Business Architect, you will collaborate closely with business leaders, the Enterprise Architect, and IT teams acting as a trusted strategic partner rather than a delivery or solution‑design role. You will play a pivotal part in bridging business priorities with IT capabilities, defining and maintaining the business architecture of the organization.

You will also operate across departmental and functional boundaries, as internal solutions are frequently extended to serve other business areas and external‑facing initiatives, requiring a consistent architectural view across the organization.

Your focus will be to ensure that business capabilities, processes, and value streams are fully aligned with our strategic objectives and supported by a coherent and future‑ready architecture landscape.

  • Develop and maintain the Business Capability Map and ensure its alignment with strategic objectives.
  • Collaborate with business stakeholders to model value streams, business processes, and operating models.
  • Facilitate workshops and discussions to identify capability gaps, redundancies, and transformation opportunities.
  • Define and maintain business architecture standards, principles
  • Map business capabilities to applications and data assets in coordination with Application/Solution Architects.
  • Support enterprise transformation, portfolio planning, and initiative prioritization using business architecture artifacts.
