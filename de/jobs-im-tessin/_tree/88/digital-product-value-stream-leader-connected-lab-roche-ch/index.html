The Position The opportunity: The Digital Product Value Stream Leader is a strategic leadership role responsible for owning the end-to-end lifecycle and commercial success of Roche Diagnostics China’s "Connected Lab"; digital portfolio. This role requires a visionary leader who will define the long-term strategic direction and vision, as well as a roadmap for connected laboratory solutions, ensuring alignment with Roche’s broader digital ecosystem and the specific needs of the Chinese market. In this capacity, you will lead, coach and empower a team of Product Managers, fostering a culture of agility, customer-centricity, and outcome-based delivery. This role serves as a critical bridge, ensuring seamless synergy between technical engineering, commercial go-to-market strategies, and customer experience, ensuring that our digital value stream maximizes laboratory efficiency, data connectivity, and diagnostic value. You will also be accountable for the P&L performance, adoption, and sustained growth of the Connected Lab product suite. You will:

  • Value Stream Strategy: Define and execute a cohesive digital stratgy for the Connected Lab value stream, transitioning products from concept to launch and maturity while ensuring seamless ineration with laboratory hardware and middleware.
  • Team Leadership: Build, coach, and lead a high-performing team of Product Managers; drive functional excellence in product disovery, backlog prioritization, and roadmap planning.
  • Lifecycle Management: Oversee the complete product lifecycle (PLM) for the portfolio, making data-driven decisions on investment, iteration, and sunsetting to maximize ROI.
  • Cross-Functional Orchestration: Act as the primary interface beween local R&D, Global teams, Commercial, and Customer Support to remove bottlenecks and ensure timely delivery of value to customers.
  • Market Success: Deeply understand the China diagnostics landscape to drive product-market fit, ensuring solutions address local regulatory requirements, workflow challenges, and digital trends. Who you are:
