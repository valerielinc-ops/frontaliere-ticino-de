Vollständige Beschreibung

Key responsibilities Are you an experienced and versatile banking lawyer who enjoys partnering with business stakeholders and advising UBS’ largest Wealth Management clients in the Global Family and Institutional Wealth (GFIW) and Ultra High Net Worth (UHNW) segment? Do you enjoy working in an entrepreneurial, international and highly collaborative environment across functions and divisions, delivering practical legal advice to internal stakeholders and finding pragmatic, commercially sound solutions? We are looking for a motivated new team colleague to:

  • Serve as a strategic legal partner to internal business stakeholders and risk functions, providing coordinated, holistic legal coverage across all matters relating to GFIW, UHNW and Strategic Clients. Advise on and facilitate the establishment of standardized and bespoke client solutions and set-ups, including the drafting, review and negotiation of legal documentation and agreements.
  • Manage pre-litigation complaint cases, advising stakeholders on strategic case management, risk assessment, dispute resolution strategies, and the drafting and negotiation of settlement agreements.
  • Validate and guide new products, services and strategic business initiatives impacting GFIW, UHNW and Strategic Clients, working closely with relevant subject matter experts. Evaluate legal and regulatory risks and mitigation measures associated with new business opportunities, innovative solutions, and bespoke transactions.
  • Advice on the global governance and risk framework applicable to GFIW, UHNW and Strategic Clients, including the development, review and maintenance of supporting documentation, agreements, and practical guidance, in close coordination across teams and jurisdictions globally.

The team You'll be working in the Legal GFIW, UHNW & Strategic Clients team in Zuerich, Lugano or Geneva. Join a team of experienced lawyers operating in a dynamic global environment across businesses, functions, and jurisdictions. We provide holistic legal support to our business partners and are committed to delivering seamless, pragmatic, and high-quality advice that enables sustainable solutions. Through close collaboration with specialist colleagues and risk functions, we ensure our clients receive coordinated, consistent, and tailored legal support. Teamwork, excellence, and a client-centric mindset are at the core of how we work.

Your skills and experience

  • Swiss law degree with admittance to the Swiss bar as an asset.
  • 3-5 years of relevant post-qualification experience in international banking, finance, or private client practice, with a strong focus on banking-related legal matters.
  • Proven experience in independently advising on complex matters, with excellent drafting and negotiation skills and a strong sense of ownership and accountability.
  • Commercially minded, with the ability to translate complex legal concepts into clear, pragmatic and actionable advice, delivering sustainable solutions for both the bank and its clients.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills, with confidence and credibility to engage effectively with senior business stakeholders and sophisticated clients.
  • Collaborative mindset with the ability to work seamlessly across teams.
  • Fluent in English and German; additional languages would be an advantage.

Brauche ich als italienischer Grenzgänger die G-Bewilligung für diese Tessiner Stelle?

Ja. Für EU/EFTA-Bürger hängt die G-Bewilligung nicht mehr von einer Grenzzone ab: Nötig sind ein Wohnsitz in einem EU/EFTA-Staat, ein Schweizer Vertrag und die Rückkehr mindestens einmal pro Woche. Die 20-km-Regel betrifft die Besteuerung, nicht die Bewilligung. Der Schweizer Arbeitgeber beantragt die G-Bewilligung nach Vertragsunterzeichnung beim Migrationsamt des Kantons Tessin — die Erstausstellung dauert 2-6 Wochen. Die Bewilligung gilt fünf Jahre bei unbefristetem oder über einjährigem Vertrag, sonst für die Vertragsdauer.
