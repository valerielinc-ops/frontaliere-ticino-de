Unternehmen und Kontext

  • The Global Data, Digital & AI (GDDA) organization plays a central role in Roche Pharma Commercial’s digital and AI ecosystem.
  • GDDA sets the enterprise digital strategy, translates commercial objectives into a coordinated portfolio of investments, and defines the standards and operating model that enable scalable execution across programs and products.
  • By connecting strategy, portfolio, standards, programs, and products, GDDA ensures that data, digital, and AI capabilities are delivered as scalable, enterprise solutions that drive adoption and improve outcomes for patients, healthcare providers, and the broader healthcare ecosystem. The Opportunity
  • The Portfolio Governance & Integration Lead is the cross-organizational coordination and governance lead for GDDA’s digital portfolio.
  • Reporting to the Head of Digital Portfolio Management & Operations, this role ensures that portfolio priorities, decisions, and trade-offs are aligned across the organizations GDDA depends on — including Pharma International (PI), Global Product Strategy (GPS) / US Commercial, Medical & Government Affairs (CMG), Roche Digital Technology (RDT), and Data, Digital, Analytics (DDA) — and that decisions made in portfolio forums translate into clear actions, owners, and follow-through.
  • This role owns the interfaces and governance mechanisms that make portfolio decisions executable.
  • It manages the relationships, cadences, decision logs, escalation paths, and cross-functional alignment required to keep portfolio priorities moving through a complex matrix.
  • The role requires strong operational discipline, stakeholder judgment, and the ability to influence without direct authority. What you will be working on Cross-functional Interface Management
  • Act as GDDA’s primary operating interface for portfolio-related coordination with PI, GPS / US CMG, RDT, and DDA.
  • Maintain active relationships with partner organizations to understand priorities, constraints, dependencies, and decision needs that affect GDDA’s portfolio.
