Vollständige Beschreibung

Do you have an analytic mindset? Are you ready to dive into the dynamic world of risk management and control at a large international investment and wealth management bank? Do you have a keen eye for detail and a curiosity for understanding how models and artificial intelligence (AI) systems align with regulatory standards and industry best practice? For our Model Risk Management & Control (MRMC) U.S. Internship Program we are looking for people like you, who can help us in transforming model risk governance and reporting:

  • •Learn how to analyze data by observing and participating in activities focused on reviewing metrics and developing summaries under close guidance
  • •Explore process improvement concepts by engaging in project-based activities involving automation and AI/visualization tools
  • •Develop an understanding of policies and procedures by shadowing initiatives related to regulatory requirements and industry standards
  • •Build skills in creating clear and impactful presentations by observing and contributing to the preparation of reporting materials
