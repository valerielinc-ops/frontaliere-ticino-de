Be a force for good and delight consumers through coffee and beverage innovation

As an R&D Specialist for System Science at Nestlé Product Technology Center for Coffee , your mission is to build the scientific foundations needed to explain, optimize and predict product, machine and packaging system performance. Our teams in Orbe, Konolfingen, Singapore and Marysville lead coffee and beverage R&D for Nestlé markets worldwide. You’ll make a difference by collaborating closely with system performance specialists, product developers, engineers and multidisciplinary research teams. This is your opportunity to take ownership, grow your expertise, and deliver meaningful results that make a difference at scale. This is your opportunity to drive cutting-edge innovation, strengthen your expertise and deliver meaningful results to create unique consumer experiences at global scale.

How you’ll make an impact as an &D Specialist - System Science

  • Formulating hypotheses that connect product, machine and packaging design choices with beverage performance.
  • Designing experiments that reveal flow, pressure, mass transfer, extraction, dissolution and foam generation mechanisms.
  • Building predictive, physics-based models that explain system behaviour and guide technical decisions.
  • Identifying performance drivers, variability and knowledge gaps to improve system robustness and optimization.
  • Creating reusable scientific knowledge that accelerates future research, product development and system innovation.
  • Translating scientific findings into clear recommendations for technical integration, validation and performance improvement.

What will make you successful

  • Bringing strong knowledge of physical chemistry, applied physics, mechanical engineering or a related scientific field.
  • Applying predictive modelling to explain system behaviour, test design choices and anticipate performance.
  • Using expertise in transport phenomena, fluid systems, experimentation and packaging to solve complex system challenges.
  • Connecting fundamental science with system design questions and communicating clearly across multidisciplinary teams.
  • Communicating fluently in English and translating complex scientific concepts into clear, engaging messages.
  • Demonstrating courage, collaboration, speed and continuous learning, while translating ideas into measurable impact and creating shared value.
