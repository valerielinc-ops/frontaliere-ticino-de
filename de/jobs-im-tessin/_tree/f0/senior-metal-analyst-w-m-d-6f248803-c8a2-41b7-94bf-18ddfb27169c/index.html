Vollständige Beschreibung

Metal Analyst (f/m/d)

Workload: 80-100%

Location: Baden, Switzerland

Join Axpo's Commodity Trading team and turn market data into insight that shapes real trading decisions. We value diverse perspectives and welcome your unique expertise.

What you will do

  • Analyze supply, demand, inventories, price trends, spreads, and key drivers in base and precious metals markets
  • Produce concise market commentary and actionable insights for trading and commercial stakeholders
  • Build and maintain models, dashboards, and datasets
  • Track relevant physical, macroeconomic, and geopolitical developments
  • Use AI tools to summarize research, automate repetitive tasks, and support first-pass analysis
  • Validate AI-generated outputs and apply sound judgment to ensure quality and accuracy

What you bring & who you are

  • Experience in commodities / metals research, market analysis, or trading support, ideally with knowledge of base, battery- and precious metals markets
  • Strong quantitative and qualitative analytical skills
  • Excellent Excel and data handling skills – Python or SQL is a plus
  • Strong written and verbal communicator with a team-oriented mindset
  • Hands-on experience using generative AI tools for summarization, drafting, or analysis support
  • Even if you don't meet all the requirements, we look forward to your application – ideally, you also bring experience with AI or machine learning for forecasting, signal generation, or document analysis

About the team

Our Commodity Trading team combines deep market expertise with a curious, collaborative mindset. We support each other, share knowledge openly, and value every perspective as we navigate fast-moving metals markets together.
