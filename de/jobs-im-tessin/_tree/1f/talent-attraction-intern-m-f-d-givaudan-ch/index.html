Talent Attraction Intern (m/f/d) - Your future position?

As a Talent Attraction Intern, you will join our CoE Talent Acquisition and People Insight team for a 12-month internship based in Vernier, Switzerland. Reporting to the Global Employer Branding & Recruitment Marketing Manager, you will support global initiatives that strengthen Givaudan's employer brand and help attract top talent across our international organisation. You will gain hands-on experience across employer branding, recruitment marketing, talent attraction campaigns, social media, talent intelligence, and candidate engagement while collaborating with stakeholders around the world.

This internship is an excellent opportunity for someone interested in Talent Acquisition, Employer Branding, Marketing, Communications, Digital Media, or Human Resources.

In this role, you will

Employer Branding & Recruitment Marketing

  • Support the development and execution of global employer branding campaigns.
  • Create and coordinate engaging content for social media, career platforms, and employer branding channels.
  • Help showcase employee stories, career opportunities, and workplace culture through internal and external communications.

Talent Attraction

  • Support talent attraction initiatives and recruitment marketing campaigns across key markets.
  • Assist with promotional activities related to careers events, university partnerships, and talent community engagement.

Digital & Social Media

  • Support content planning and performance tracking across employer branding channels.
  • Assist in measuring campaign effectiveness and preparing insights and recommendations.
  • Explore the use of AI and emerging technologies to enhance talent attraction and candidate engagement.

Project & Team Support

  • Prepare presentations, reports, and communication materials for global projects.
  • Support coordination of cross-functional employer branding and recruitment marketing initiatives.
  • Contribute ideas that improve candidate experience and attraction effectiveness.

Your profile includes

  • Currently pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's degree in Marketing, Communications, Human Resources, Business Administration, Psychology, or a related field.
  • Strong written and verbal communication skills in English.
  • Strong organisational skills and attention to detail.
  • Interest in Employer Branding, Talent Acquisition, Recruitment Marketing, or Talent Attraction.
  • Proactive mindset and eagerness to learn.
  • Ability to work collaboratively in an international environment.
  • First experience using social media platforms for content creation or campaign promotion preferred
  • Familiarity with creative tools, analytics platforms, or content management tools.
  • Interest in digital marketing, talent intelligence, and AI applications.

What You'll Gain

During this internship, you will gain exposure to

  • Global employer branding strategy.
  • Recruitment marketing and talent attraction campaigns.
  • Social media and digital content creation.
  • Employer branding analytics and performance measurement.
  • AI and emerging technologies in Talent Acquisition.
  • Collaboration with global teams and stakeholders.
