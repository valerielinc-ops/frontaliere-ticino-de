Who You Are

You bring enthusiasm for fashion and thrive in a dynamic retail environment. As a Michael Kors Ambassador, you represent the brand’s values with professionalism and integrity. You’re committed to creating inclusive, welcoming experiences for all customers, using strong communication, teamwork, and a proactive mindset to navigate challenges with energy and optimism.

What You’ll Do

 Lead by example as a MK Ambassador, ensuring exceptional customer experiences to drive loyalty and repeat business.  Maintain client communication using all available clientelling apps and tools.  Maintain floor and stock presence.  Build and maintain effective communication with the management team to drive store performance.  Use of Product Knowledge packs to become a Selling Expert to advise our customers with passion and confidence.  Understand business results and opportunities to drive sales.

You’ll Need to Have

 Excitement and enthusiasm to work in fast paced retail environment with relevant sales experience.  Capability and desire to collaborate and inspire all fellow team players to work together to achieve results.  Technological proficiency, with the ability to leverage technology to drive sales and customer experience.  Local language preferable and effective communication skills.

What We Offer

 Competitive compensation package  Attractive commission scheme  Clothing allowance
